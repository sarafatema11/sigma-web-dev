Container properies:

CSS Flexbox:

ye jo flexbox hai wo container pr lagayi jati hai individual item pr nahi lagayi jati. "Ek container jis k under multiple items ho".

  1. display: flex;

    2. biodefault flexbox aap k item ko left to right ker deta hai automatically means (vertically to horizontal).

  2. justify-content:center;

    3. item "center hojayega "

  3. align-item:center;

    4. ye use krne se vertically "center hojayega."

    justify-content properties:

    ye sab property ka use ker k aap items ko "horizontally" move ker sakte ho.

    1. justify-content:center;

      2. items "center hojayega."

    2. justify-content:flex-start;

      3. ye flex ki start ki taraf chala jayega.

    3. justify-content:flex-end;

      4. ye flex ki end ki taraf chala jayega.

    4. justify-content:right;

      5. ye item ko "right isde" move kr dega.

    5. justify-content:left;

      6. ye item ko "left isde" move kr dega.

    6. justify-content:space-around;

      7. starting se ending (equal soace divided kerta) mein space ajayega.

    7. justify-content:space-between;

      8. only item k between space dega starting se ending me space nahi dega.

    8. space-evvenly;

      9. means barabar space krna.

    9. difference btwn SPACE-AROUND / SPACR-BETWEEN:

      10. space around: equally divided space.
      space between: starting & ending me space nhi hoga sirf beech me hpga.
