Css Position Property:

  1. Static:

    2. (biodefault property of position)
    jaha pr tha waha rahega koi aasar nahi padega. top bottom etc nahi laga sakte..

  2. position: relative; :

    3. jo itni ki aap ni jagha thi waha mein top:4px; laga sakta hu. isse element ko move ker sakta ho.
    z-index:1; tabhi kaam karega jab positiion: relative laga hua ho.
    is mein Aap ya to top / bottom or left / right in dono me se koi ek laga sakte ho.

    example:

    top:34px;
    left:56px;

  3. z-index:1; :

    4. ye laga ne se hum ek dusre box ko upr neeche kr sakte hai. example box 1 dekho

  4. position: absolute; :

    5. ye lagta hai "Nearest Positioned ancestor"

    example: (box 1 & parent ka style css dekho.)

    position: Absolute;
    top : 0px;
    left : 0px;

    jo position Absolute hai wo aapne "nearest parent" dundti hai jo ki (Positioned)hai.

    or ek baar wo mil gaya aapna "nearest Positioned element" us k relative wo top & left / bottom & right / bottom & left / top & right etc. us k hisab se Aap ne position ko laga deti hai.

  5. positiion: Fixed; :

    6. Aap ki position ko fixed kerdega Aap scroll bhi karoge to wo fixed hi rahega remove nhi hogga.

    example: (box 3 style css dekho )

    position: fixed;
    bottom: 0px;
    left:8vw;
    height: 111px;

  6. Sticky Position:

    7. sticky means jipak jane wali cheez jab hum kisi bhi element ko / ya somethhing else ko sticky position de dete hai to wo vahi chipak jata hai.
    fixed & sticky both are different.

    example: (see box 1 style css)

    position: sticky;
    top: 0px;
    height: 111px;
    width: 100%;
    margin: 0;
    padding: 0;

    Differencw btwn Fixed & Sticky:

      2. means fixed kerdo ek jagah per.

    2. Sticky:

      3. means jab tak Aap is ki above scroll nahi karoge ye static rahhega or jaise hi is k par scroll karoge dewar k par wo sticky ban jayega.
