# Exercise 13: Dynamic Website Builder

**Objective:** JavaScript ki help se screenshot jaisa YouTube video card design karna hai.

### Question:

Ek function `createCard()` complete karo jo har baar call hone par ek naya video card create kare aur HTML ke `.container` ke andar append kare.

Function ko ye 6 parameters milenge:

1. `title` – Video ka title
2. `cName` – Channel ka naam
3. `views` – Total views (integer)
4. `monthsold` – Kitne months pehle upload hua
5. `duration` – Video duration (string)
6. `thumbnail` – Thumbnail image URL

### Requirements:

1. JavaScript se dynamically video card create karo.
2. Card mein thumbnail image show karo.
3. Thumbnail par video duration show karo.
4. Card mein video title show karo.
5. Channel name, views aur upload time show karo.
6. Card ko `.container` ke andar append karo.
7. Jab bhi `createCard()` call ho, ek naya card add hona chahiye.
8. Multiple function calls par multiple cards show hone chahiye.

### Expected Output:

Diye gaye screenshot jaisa YouTube video card design banana hai.

**Condition:** HTML mein har video card manually nahi banana hai. Saara card JavaScript ke through dynamically generate hona chahiye.
